Take the Stars: three ways a round could feel

Grey boxes, 3–5 minutes each, five seeds. Judge one thing: after a round, do you want the next seed, and do you know why?

APours
My Core holds three spirits. Each pour is a different spark; the right spark at the right moment wins.
  • Read the colour gathering at a rival Core, then answer it.
  • Try winning with one kind only; it should fail.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
 BTides
The sky tells me what's coming. I commit my swarm at the right moment, or hold it.
  • Watch the track; hold the pool for the Eclipse.
  • Lure a rival into a Starfall lane.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
 CClaims
My swarm is stardust. I spend it to claim the sky, or throw it at their Core.
  • Plant a Beacon on the Well before anyone.
  • Bastion the choke, then strike the other way.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
