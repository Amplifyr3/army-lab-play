POURS

Your Core holds three star spirits. Each pour is a different kind of spark, and the right spark at the right moment wins.

Tap a spirit, then the board: the whole pool pours as that kind. Tap a rival Core to assault it, your own to call everyone home. Hold a spirit for its power. Gold star = attuned (+30%).Tap a spirit, then tap the board: your whole pool pours out as that kind. Tap a rival Core to assault it. Tap your own Core to call everyone home. Hold a spirit for its power (glowing pip = ready): Ember bombs your last tap, Moss rushes, Tide mends walls. The gold star marks your attuned spirit (+30%).

What to try
1. Watch a rival Core: the colour gathering there says what is coming. Answer it.
2. Try winning with one kind only. It should fail.
3. Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
