Take the Stars: ways a round could feel

Grey boxes, 3–5 minutes each, five seeds. Judge one thing: after a round, do you want the next seed, and do you know why?

E2Flight with a rival
My spirit flies the champion's craft against a rival with a temperament: Ram jousts, Kite circles, Wisp slips away. Same dice, same Strain, and it never sees my path.
  • Watch its engine lean in Plan, then check its read after the reveal.
  • Find each rival's weak spot: Ram's tail, Kite's circling side, Wisp's Strain after a Flip.
  • Lose once and read the end card: does it say why?
 D2Lanes
Three of my spirits each lead a squadron. I send them down the right route at the right moment with one gesture, and the sky holds its breath while I decide.
  • Hold a squad: time slows and its routes light up. Flick to go.
  • Read their arrows; brace a Pike where a charge is told.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
 DSquadrons
My spirits each lead a squadron. I slide them with my fingers; how I move them decides the fight.
  • Brace Pikes and let a charge hit the bristle.
  • Charge their Archers from the flank.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
 APours
My Core holds three spirits. Each pour is a different spark; the right spark at the right moment wins.
  • Read the colour gathering at a rival Core, then answer it.
  • Try winning with one kind only; it should fail.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
 BTides
The sky tells me what's coming. I commit my swarm at the right moment, or hold it.
  • Watch the track; hold the pool for the Eclipse.
  • Lure a rival into a Starfall lane.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
 CClaims
My swarm is stardust. I spend it to claim the sky, or throw it at their Core.
  • Plant a Beacon on the Well before anyone.
  • Bastion the choke, then strike the other way.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
