Take the Stars: ways a round could feel

Grey boxes, 3–5 minutes each, five seeds. Judge one thing: after a round, do you want the next seed, and do you know why?

I7Arena: clear the board
Total domination is yours to take: the only win is breaking both rival Cores yourself, and each one is its own moment. SCORPIUS FALLS, the Core implodes, and its land greys and rolls into yours from the Core out. The last one floods the board while the camera pulls back. There is no timer: from 3:00 their walls crumble every 30 s, and at 5:00 their turrets go dark, so every match ends in about four to six minutes. Your mech claims land as it flies, so you never wait for your line. The upgrade cards are big and still, each shows its before and after, and your mech changes the moment you pick. With a titan near, the second button turns gold: tap BREAK three times and it staggers, whichever mech you fly.
  • Fly straight at Scorpius: the wake paints your colour under you and your waves catch up.
  • Break a Core and watch its land roll in. Break the second and watch the whole board flood.
  • Tap BREAK on a titan three times: three plates, a stagger, then unload.
 I6Arena: dominance
The board is a triangle now: a base in each corner, a Titan Forge on each edge, the Heart at the centre. Every KO drops XP; each level, time slows and you tap one of three upgrade cards that change your mech. Titans wear armour that only your Ram, Bulwark or Seed Turret cracks, and a crack staggers them. Land cut off from its base goes grey and is swept up, land held 25 s is fortified, and your base holds itself. Win and the board floods to you: TOTAL DOMINANCE, nine seconds where everything dies in one hit, then a compact strip.
  • Hold 55% for 5 s and watch the flood roll out, the Cores implode one by one, and the mop-up.
  • Ram (or wall, or plant your turret on) a titan: a plate cracks off and it staggers, take it down in about 20 s.
  • Cut a corridor through a rival's land: the far side greys and your sweep takes it.
 I5Arena: the tide
Three crews now, so you have two long front lines and every clash you fly into is the big one. Each base pours packs of ten behind a herald that hold the line in dense rows, trading tracers. Carve into a line near you: 14 KOs and it breaks open, your tide floods through and the land surges to you in a wave. Matches last 4:00, or end sooner when you hold 55% for 5 s (DOMINATION). Combos, streaks and OVERCHARGE drops keep coming, and nearly every win unlocks something on the end card.
  • Fly to the front facing Scorpius and fill the BREAK bar over their line: watch it crack, flood and surge.
  • Kill a titan or break a turret, then fly over the gold OVERCHARGE orb.
  • Win your first match: Moss unlocks, and the card flips over.
 I4Arena: one mech, clear orders
One mech per crew, picked on the start card. Tap the map (top left) for command mode: time slows to 15% and one or two taps give your waves a standing order, RALLY, ATTACK, DEFEND or FOLLOW ME. Bases lose the dome and the big gun: three small turrets reach exactly as far as your guns, and the Core opens once all three are down. Everyone has an overshield, enemy land no longer drains you, respawns grow with each death, and the view is wider with no fog and no rocks.
  • Tap the map, tap ATTACK, tap a rival's land: watch your dotted road and your waves swing there.
  • Pick off a turret from the edge of your range, then the next: the other two can't reach you there.
  • Take a hit and watch the shield: three quiet seconds and it is back.
 I3Arena: six fronts
Six crews, one at each corner of the hex star. Each base pours waves that man its front line and push it, so every ship has a job: marching, holding or pushing. Land is the solid ground behind your front. You are the titan who breaks their line so your waves surge through. Real titans walk out of every base now and then. Hold the ring round the Heart and your waves double. The camera sits further out and the top of the screen is nearly empty.
  • Fly to a front and watch your waves follow you there and push.
  • When a rival titan comes your way, stop it before it breaks your line.
  • Take the ring round the Heart and hold it.
 I2Arena: the hexagram
The arena on a hexagonal board of hex cells. Three crews sit on one triangle of a hexagram and three star beacons on the other. Held, a beacon grows land and is a place to swap and relaunch. Hold all three and the Heart at the centre burns, and your sparks surge. The rivals are more aggressive now. A 4-crew layout is on the start card.
  • Take the beacon beside your base early, then watch a rival come for it.
  • Raid a rival dome and see who hits your base while you're away.
  • Try 4 crews: two opposite pairs fighting over two beacons.
 IArena: four crews, one star system
The mission's spirits and bases on an open board, back to zone control. Four crews, a base in each corner. Fly anywhere, flip space to my colour, and break the rival Cores. My land spreads on its own and feeds my base. Camera C, locked. Auto-aim or dual stick, picked on the start card.
  • Spend the first minute flipping open space beside your land, then raid the weakest base: the minimap shows who is where and what they intend.
  • Break a gun from under its dome, then the Core behind it: that crew's land becomes yours.
  • Try a match in dual stick: the right stick aims and fires, a quick tap there dashes.
 H2Mission: a camera lab
The same sortie with four locked cameras to compare, switched live by the CAM chip (top left) or on the start card. A is high and forward with a bigger mech and a fog ahead; B is side-on, progress to the right; C is diagonal; D is H's low chase. Speed streaks, parallax and a zoom kick on DASH in all four.
  • Fly sector 1 in A, then tap CAM and fly sector 2 in B: which one feels fast?
  • Run in on Gate 1 in A: the volley's red chevron warns you from off-screen.
  • Gate 2's second battery is weaker once the first falls; the heart's waves flare first.
 HMission: take a star
A sortie up one star's system, played facing the star: mow its swarms, break the batteries that guard its gates, duel its guardian, then hold the heart until the star lights in my colour. Landscape; the camera faces forward and never turns.
  • Antares: let a battery's volley chase you, then run in under the dome and DASH its flak ring.
  • Thuban: hide behind a wreck from the sweep beam; duel Draco and step out of his Breath Beam.
  • Kakkab: lead the missiles into the ice; Lupus lunges, and at 30 % he hunts you three times.
 G4Musou: three lanes
The musou fight on a real board. Three lanes run from my base to theirs, two towers a side on each, a jungle between with buff camps, and a Leviathan from 3:00. Towers stay down, so every one I take is progress. Rivals come back slower each time they fall, and the minimap shows what each one is up to.
  • Walk your sparks into a tower first; hit a rival under it and the tower turns on you.
  • After a double kill, look at their respawn timers: that is your window for a tower.
  • Try each spirit's second ability: Ember's ram, Tide's wall, Moss's turret.
 F4Tactics: three ways to fly
F3's combat with three new controls to compare, picked on the start card. Stick: a flight-stick pad whose edges are the ship's limits; up is faster, side is turn, lift locks. Rehearse: fly the ghost with your thumb for two seconds and keep exactly what you flew. Chase: the same from a cockpit behind your ship, then the camera pulls back to show the path.
  • Try each for a match: which one feels like flying, which like control?
  • Every input is legal: no stiffening, no "close enough".
  • Hold the Viper hard at the edge at speed 2 to loop.
 E2Flight with a rival
My spirit flies the champion's craft against a rival with a temperament: Ram jousts, Kite circles, Wisp slips away. Same dice, same Strain, and it never sees my path.
  • Watch its engine lean in Plan, then check its read after the reveal.
  • Find each rival's weak spot: Ram's tail, Kite's circling side, Wisp's Strain after a Flip.
  • Lose once and read the end card: does it say why?
 D2Lanes
Three of my spirits each lead a squadron. I send them down the right route at the right moment with one gesture, and the sky holds its breath while I decide.
  • Hold a squad: time slows and its routes light up. Flick to go.
  • Read their arrows; brace a Pike where a charge is told.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
 DSquadrons
My spirits each lead a squadron. I slide them with my fingers; how I move them decides the fight.
  • Brace Pikes and let a charge hit the bristle.
  • Charge their Archers from the flank.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
 APours
My Core holds three spirits. Each pour is a different spark; the right spark at the right moment wins.
  • Read the colour gathering at a rival Core, then answer it.
  • Try winning with one kind only; it should fail.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
 BTides
The sky tells me what's coming. I commit my swarm at the right moment, or hold it.
  • Watch the track; hold the pool for the Eclipse.
  • Lure a rival into a Starfall lane.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
 CClaims
My swarm is stardust. I spend it to claim the sky, or throw it at their Core.
  • Plant a Beacon on the Well before anyone.
  • Bastion the choke, then strike the other way.
  • Play seeds 1 and 2 back to back.
