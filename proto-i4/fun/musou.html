Musou G4: three lanes

Three lanes run from your base (left) to theirs (right), two towers a side on each, a jungle between. Break one lane's towers and their base tower and their Core opens.

  • Left thumb: the stick, anywhere on the left. A full push is full speed; let go and you hover.
  • Right thumb: SPECIAL, DASH (dodges a red shape), and your spirit's own second ability.
  • Towers shoot your sparks first. Hit a rival under one and it turns on you: let your sparks walk in first.
  • Jungle camps give a short buff; the Leviathan (from 3:00) empowers your lanes. Tap the minimap to look around.
