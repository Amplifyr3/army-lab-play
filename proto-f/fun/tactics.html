Tactics: six ships, one timeline

Three spirits against three rivals, three battles in a row. Everyone plans in initiative order, nobody moves until all have planned, then it all plays out at once, and the cards flip.

  • Draw a ship's path on the board, from the ship. Loops are fine. The ring is its budget; lift to lock it in.
  • It faces where the path ends. Where everyone ends decides which cards light up: in arc, tailing, head-on.
  • Combat: 5 cards, 3 Energy. Tap a lit card, tap a target. Rivals show what they intend; answer it.
  • Two fingers peek at the whole board. Win a battle, pick a card.
