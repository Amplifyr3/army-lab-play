Tactics: six ships, one timeline

Three ships against three rivals, one match. Plan in initiative order, fly in initiative order, then the cards flip and each ship gives one order. High initiative plans last and shoots first.

  • Plan each ship's move with the pad below the board. The pad is the ship's legal move space: speed changes one step a turn, and it can only turn so fast, so whatever you set is exactly what it flies. The fans are where ships can reach.
  • Orders: zoom in on each ship and tap a target in its arc to fire; that is always free. Tap a boost card first if you want one. Rivals order in secret.
  • Fireworks: everything resolves high initiative first. A ship destroyed before its turn never fires.
