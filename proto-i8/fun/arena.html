Arena I8: the build

Three crews on a triangle of the hexagram: a base in each corner, a Titan Forge on each edge, the Heart at the centre. You fly the mech that claims land as it flies, breaks their lines, levels up, and breaks every Core.

YouScorpiusDraco
  • Win: break both rival Cores yourself: clear the board. Lose: your Core falls. From 3:00 their walls crumble (the Siege); at 7:00 their Cores give way.
  • Level up every few seconds: tap one of three cards. Weapons orbit and fire round your mech; take one to III with its partner and the gold card evolves it. A titan near you lights your second button gold: one tap of BREAK opens it and your sparks pile in.
  • Hold a forge's ring and it builds you titans. Land held 25 s is fortified; land cut off from its base goes grey. Tap the map (top left) for orders.
